**Prerequisites:** a verified account with `report:review` and an accepted MFA session. A scenario operator is not automatically a report reviewer.

<Steps><Step>
### Open the queue

Visit `/dashboard/moderation`. The server lists pending reports; empty means no pending records returned, while a failure offers Retry. Source/API pagination must be qualified against the intended deployment before release.
</Step><Step>
### Review privately

Read category, observation time, location, note and available media within authorized access. Treat citizen content as a report, not a validated measurement. Do not copy private details into public explanations.
</Step><Step>
### Record a decision

Choose approved or rejected and provide a nonblank review reason (maximum2,000 characters). The API locks the report, allows transition only from pending, and records actor, before/after state and reason in an audit event.
</Step></Steps>

## Expected outcome

A successful decision removes the item from the pending queue. Approval permits only the allowlisted, privacy-reduced community projection. A second decision on an already-reviewed report returns409; reload the queue rather than assuming your original decision was lost. Missing/unauthorized resources are not disclosed through owner endpoints.

<Callout title="No automatic scientific endorsement">Moderation is a publication-control workflow. It does not establish laboratory accuracy, pollutant causation or plant-control advice.</Callout>
