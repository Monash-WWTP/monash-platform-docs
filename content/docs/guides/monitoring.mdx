**Prerequisites:** an environment serving the versioned monitoring API. No scenario permission is needed to read the current public monitoring contract.

<Steps><Step>
### Find a location

Open `/dashboard`, search a plant name or code, and select the list entry or map marker. Map failure does not remove the list. On phones, the laboratory panel behaves as a labelled dialog; Escape closes it and focus returns to the previous control.
</Step><Step>
### Establish context

Read station code, sampling point, source year and latest available sample date. “Latest” means the latest sample in the selected records, not a live reading or current plant-health claim.
</Step><Step>
### Filter and inspect

Choose a parameter beside the latest result. Open **Filter records** to choose year and dataset-reported status; the closed section still shows active filters. Open **Summary** for quantified/total counts and **Recent Samples** for individual rows. The trend remains visible. Mean/max exclude missing and BDL values; a measured numeric zero remains valid. A BDL result such as `&lt;2 mg/L` states a detection threshold, not a concentration of zero or exactly two.
</Step></Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Do not infer permits or influent loads">Dataset-reported “Comply” is retained from the source. No station-specific permit assessment is established. Effluent BOD/nitrogen cannot be substituted for the influent data required by GHG accounting.</Callout>

## Expected outcome and recovery

You can identify the date/source and the usable observations behind a statistic. No matching samples is an empty selection; service failure is an unavailable state with Retry that retains filters. Open **Methods and research** for accounting limitations and the Research sandbox action. Research is available only when the sampling location has a linked treatment plant.
