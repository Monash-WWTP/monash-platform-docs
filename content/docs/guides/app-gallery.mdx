These are **actual Android emulator captures of the current source preview**, taken on 19 October 2026. They are not mockups and do not represent the downloadable public APK 1.0.0. The source app uses the platform's original green theme; this manual keeps its neutral theme.

The fresh Pixel 7 emulator ran Android API 36 at 1080 × 2400. It was signed out and connected to a GET-only local fixture with two synthetic observations near Kuala Lumpur. No real citizen accounts, private reports or photos appear. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Screenshots were encoded as lossless WebP without retouching.

## What to look for

- **Report:** four distinct categories, fixed units for numeric readings and a three-state wastewater condition selector. Semantic warning/critical colours remain alongside text and icons.
- **Supporting details:** photo/note controls expand on request; location and Submit remain part of the form. Some captures show the genuine location-acquisition state, while others show captured GPS.
- **Map:** the observation layer and Recent readings disclosure keep navigation visible. The title currently says “Live map”; it is a fetched observation view, not a continuous sensor feed. The detail sheet shows a synthetic approved public record without private notes/media.
- **Me:** signed-out shared-account entry and privacy context. The external identity browser, signed-in history and successful photo submission are not represented by these captures and still require integrated release qualification.

Use [reporting](/docs/guides/reporting), [mobile map and account](/docs/guides/mobile-map-and-account) and [current availability](/docs/overview/status) for behavior and release boundaries. Open an image to inspect its original resolution. Capture hashes and APK/source provenance are recorded in the documentation repository's `reference/mobile-interface-capture.json`.
