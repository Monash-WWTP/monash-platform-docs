<Callout title="Current source workflow">This guide describes the shared-API source client. The signed public 1.0.0 app remains on its legacy backend; verify the actual release/environment before expecting this identity behavior.</Callout>

The bottom navigation has **Report**, **Map** and **Me**. The map title says “Live map”, but that label does not establish continuous measurements or a validated real-time plant feed.

<Steps><Step>
### Read public observations and your own recent reports

Open Map in the shared-API source. The repository first loads approved `/api/v1/community/observations`; when signed in, it also loads your own `/api/v1/reports`. It merges records by ID, with your own record replacing its public version. Public locations are rounded; your own authorized records may include private details. Each request initially asks for up to 200 records, so this is not a complete paginated historical archive. Refresh reloads the list; displayed results are not live sensor telemetry.
</Step><Step>
### Inspect a report detail

Tap a map marker, or expand **Recent readings** and choose a row (up to 20 rows are displayed). The scrollable detail sheet shows category, reading/condition, local display time, optional note/photo and pending-review status. Photo content is fetched through authorized media access; unavailable media displays Photo unavailable. Your own pending/private content is not evidence it is publicly visible.
</Step><Step>
### Manage your session

Open Me. Sign in uses the configured external identity browser and shows the verified account email when available. A citizen account does not grant operator access. Sign out clears locally stored refresh/ID credentials and attempts provider end-session; broader revocation must be verified against the intended identity environment.
</Step></Steps>

## Offline and service errors

Use the map refresh/retry control after a transient failure. Refresh-token provider errors preserve the stored identity rather than erasing it automatically; reconnect or sign in again as instructed. The source does not establish an unlimited offline report queue, complete tile caching or background synchronization guarantee.

Public approved observations are a different API projection with rounded locations and no private note/media. See [community observations](/docs/guides/community-observations). Account recovery/email verification depend on the identity provider and remain an integrated release gate, not a native-app password-reset screen supplied by this source.
