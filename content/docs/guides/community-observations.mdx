Open `/dashboard` and use **Approved community wastewater** to show or hide the public community layer. These are citizen reports reviewed by a moderator. They are not laboratory samples or automatically ingested simulation inputs.

## What the public projection includes

The v1 compatibility API allowlists category and condition/reading fields; v2 exposes protocol-qualified observation fields appropriate to its public contract, rounded location and observation timestamps. It excludes private identity, free-text notes, exact original coordinates and media paths. Rounded map positions must not be used to identify the original reporting location.

<Steps><Step>
### Check provenance

Read the reported condition or observation type and date. V2 visual presence/appearance observations and instrument readings have different meaning and provenance. Normal/warning/critical describe the citizen's reported wastewater condition, not an automatically measured plant alert.
</Step><Step>
### Handle absence correctly

An empty approved list means no observations are available under this contract. It does not prove no incidents occurred. On an API failure, use Retry community observations. The map/list fallback remains distinct from the lab panel.
</Step></Steps>

## What approval means

Approval controls public projection. It is not a laboratory verification, proof of causation or authorization to publish the underlying private report/photo. See [moderation](/docs/guides/moderation) for the review process.
