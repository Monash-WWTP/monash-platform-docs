A verified account needs `report:respond` and MFA to use `/dashboard/operations`. Review capability and scenario capability do not automatically grant operational response access.

The queue supports unassigned, assigned, investigating and closed states. Open a report to inspect its private content and response history. Choose an eligible responder, associate an approved registry plant or station, record inspection evidence or action, and close with a disposition. Reopen when further work is needed.

Each action requires a reason and the expected response revision. Inspection records a result (confirmed, not confirmed or unable to verify), a timezone-aware observation time and evidence/method note. Action and closure require a note. A concurrent update returns 409; reload and review the new history before retrying.

The event log preserves who acted and the recorded reason/context. Operational closure does not approve a report for publication, certify a measurement or authorize model decision use. Keep sensitive details within authorized workflows.
