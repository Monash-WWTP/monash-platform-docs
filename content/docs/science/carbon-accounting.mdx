## Current implemented arithmetic

Let B be assumed influent BOD₅ in mg/L, N be influent TKN in mg N/L, fCH4 in kg CH₄/kg BOD₅, fN2O in kg N₂O-N/kg N, and recovery terms rCH4/rN2O in kg gas/m³.

```text
I_CH4 = (B × fCH4 × 10⁻³ − rCH4) × 28
I_N2O = (N × fN2O × (44/28) × 10⁻³ − rN2O) × 265
I_total = I_CH4 + I_N2O                         [kg CO₂-eq/m³]
E_daily = I_total × flow_MLD × 1,000            [kg CO₂-eq/day]
```

mg/L to kg/m³ is 10⁻³; 44/28 converts N₂O-N to N₂O mass. The daily model applies the demand concentration multiplier to BOD/TKN before accounting. Factors and recovery do not depend on aeration or maintenance.

## Hand-checked synthetic example

For B=250, N=40, generic factors 0.0121/0.0093 and zero recovery:

- CH₄: `250 × .0121 × .001 × 28 = .0847 kg CO₂-eq/m³`.
- N₂O: `40 × .0093 × (44/28) × .001 × 265 ≈ .154911 kg CO₂-eq/m³`.
- Total: approximately `.239611 kg CO₂-eq/m³` before model demand/rounding effects.

These are arithmetic examples, not measured plant intensities.

## Source and unresolved interpretation

[CUWA/IWA 2024 guideline](https://doi.org/10.2166/9781789064223), revised Eqs. 5.25/5.28 and Tables 5.6/5.8, supports the generic integrated centralized-aerobic factors 0.0121/0.0093 under its convention. GWP 28/265 is that 100-year convention, not a claim of the latest universal values.

The printed methane equation places recovery before a later 10⁻³ while defining recovery in kg/m³. The helper subtracts after converting gross mass: unit-consistent, but not literal printed placement. Author/publisher clarification remains open. The nitrogen equation defines Kjeldahl input while appendix language refers to total nitrogen; preserve the explicit TKN assumption. Ammonia or final effluent cannot substitute silently.

Recovery is **not clamped at zero**. Excessive assumed recovery can produce negative results; this behavior is not validation of a negative inventory. Establish plausible, evidenced boundaries before any inventory use.

## Factor provenance gaps

SBR/AAO/AO/oxidation-ditch/aerobic-tank rows match the cited edition numerically, subject to process mapping. ANAMMOX methane `.02` and AGS methane `.0033` are unsupported by the cited methane table; their duplication of N₂O values is not evidence of correct methane factors. The audit did not inspect external live factor assignments.

This helper is a limited process accounting calculation, not a full carbon inventory. Do not mix its factors/boundaries with [IPCC 2019 methods](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2019rf/pdf/5_Volume5/19R_V5_6_Ch06_Wastewater.pdf) without an explicit methodological review.
