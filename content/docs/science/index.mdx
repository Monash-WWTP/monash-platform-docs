## What rigor means here

Rigor means exposing the actual algorithm and unresolved evidence, not adding citations to make unsupported predictions appear authoritative. Generic accounting factors have traceable guideline entries; empirical effluent coefficients lack equation-level provenance and site calibration. The existing source audit is a claim-by-claim fact check, not a systematic review or independent model approval.

Every synthetic example in this manual describes arithmetic/software behavior. A successful regression test establishes that behavior, not the validity of its environmental interpretation.
