## Time and input resolution

Horizons are30,90 or180 days, starting at the requested UTC date. Demand percentage takes precedence when supplied: `m = max(0, 1 + demand_change_pct/100)`. Otherwise the model uses a custom truthy multiplier or low/normal/high values0.85/1/1.25.

```text
weekly(i) = 1 + 0.06 × sin(2πi/7)
flow(i) = assumed_flow × m × weekly(i)          [MLD]
concentration_multiplier = sqrt(m)
hydraulic_capacity(i) = capacity_mld × pump_availability(i)/100
utilization(i) = flow(i) / max(hydraulic_capacity(i), 10⁻⁶)
```

Maintenance applies on `start_date ≤ day < start_date + duration_days` and lowers the unit's availability to the minimum active event/baseline value. Weather and rainfall are stored but do not drive these equations.

## Four bounded surrogate updates

For each day, let `a` be aeration fraction, `h` the nonnegative hydraulic ratio, `o = 1/(1+max(h−1,0))`, `e=min(h,4)` and `dt=0.25` day. Four updates move BOD/COD/TSS/ammonia/organic nitrogen toward their assumed influent with `state += dt × e × (incoming−state)`. Nitrate is exchanged toward zero. Nitrification activity relaxes toward aeration using coefficient0.45.

BOD decays by `exp(−dt × (7a+0.2) × o)`; COD uses `(4a+0.2)`. Organic nitrogen mineralization uses0.3. Ammonia nitrification uses2.5 times activity and overload factor, with0.85 nitrate yield. Denitrification is bounded by available nitrate and a carbon-dependent expression using0.8/0.4; BOD decreases by0.25 times the denitrified amount. States are nonnegative and activity is capped at1.

## Derived outputs and screens

```text
TSS_out = TSS_state × (1 − 0.95 × clarifier_fraction)
P_out = max(4.5 × (1 − 0.75 × clarifier_fraction) + 0.8 + 1.5 × overload, 0.3)
turbidity = max(0.55 × TSS_out + 1, 0.5)
pH = 7.2 − 0.30 × (1 − aeration_fraction)
```

Daily exceedance is output greater than its configured/fallback limit. The combined screen counts days where any checked metric exceeds its limit; pH is skipped in that KPI loop. Fallback values are BOD50, COD200, TSS100mg/L. Mean capacity utilization is capped at200% in its KPI, so the KPI is not an uncapped physical capacity measurement.

<Callout type="warn" title="These are implementation equations, not validated process laws">The states are concentration surrogates, not ASM1 stoichiometric states or a reactor mass balance. MLD capacity is not reactor volume/HRT. The coefficients, weekly waveform and derived formulas have no supplied site calibration or independent validation; do not assign physical rate units/transferability without supporting evidence.</Callout>
