## Time and input resolution

Horizons are 30, 90 or 180 days, starting at the requested UTC date. Demand percentage takes precedence when supplied: `m = max(0, 1 + demand_change_pct/100)`. Otherwise the model uses a custom truthy multiplier or low/normal/high values 0.85/1/1.25.

```text
weekly(i) = 1 + 0.06 × sin(2πi/7)
flow(i) = assumed_flow × m × weekly(i)          [MLD]
concentration_multiplier = sqrt(m)
hydraulic_capacity(i) = capacity_mld × pump_availability(i)/100
utilization(i) = flow(i) / max(hydraulic_capacity(i), 10⁻⁶)
```

Maintenance applies on `start_date ≤ day < start_date + duration_days` and lowers the unit's availability to the minimum active event/baseline value. Weather and rainfall are stored but do not drive these equations.

## Four bounded surrogate updates

Initial concentrations use the **unscaled** assumed influent: nonnegative BOD, COD, TSS and ammonia; organic nitrogen is `max(TKN − ammonia, 0)`. Nitrate starts at zero and nitrification activity `A` at one. Daily incoming concentrations are multiplied by `sqrt(m)`.

For each day, `a` and `c` are aeration and clarifier availability fractions. Let `h = max(utilization, 0)`, `o = 1/(1 + max(h − 1, 0))`, `e = min(h, 4)` and `dt = 0.25`. Apply the following sequence four times per day; each expression uses the state updated by preceding lines.

```text
A += dt × 0.45 × (a − A)
X += dt × e × (X_in − X)    # each of BOD, COD, TSS, ammonia, organic N
nitrate += dt × e × (−nitrate)
BOD *= exp(−dt × (7a + 0.2) × o)
COD *= exp(−dt × (4a + 0.2) × o)
mineralized = organic_N × (1 − exp(−dt × 0.3 × o))
organic_N -= mineralized
ammonia += mineralized
nitrified = ammonia × (1 − exp(−dt × 2.5 × A × o))
ammonia -= nitrified
nitrate += 0.85 × nitrified
denitrified = min(nitrate,
  dt × 0.8 × (1 − 0.4a) × o × nitrate × min(BOD / max(BOD_in, 1), 1))
nitrate -= denitrified
BOD = max(BOD − 0.25 × denitrified, 0)
```

After each substep, clamp every concentration and activity to at least zero, and cap activity at one. The daily overload term used below is `clip(utilization − 1, 0, 1)`. BOD, COD, ammonia and nitrate outputs are the final states; TSS receives the clarifier adjustment below.

## Derived outputs and screens

```text
TSS_out = TSS_state × (1 − 0.95 × clarifier_fraction)
P_out = max(4.5 × (1 − 0.75 × clarifier_fraction) + 0.8 + 1.5 × overload, 0.3)
turbidity = max(0.55 × TSS_out + 1, 0.5)
pH = 7.2 − 0.30 × (1 − aeration_fraction)
```

Daily exceedance is output greater than its configured/fallback limit. The combined screen counts days where any checked metric exceeds its limit; pH is skipped in that KPI loop. Fallback values are BOD 50, COD 200, TSS 100 mg/L. The combined percentage is `100 × (1 − mean(daily_any_exceedance))`. For capacity utilization, clip **each daily ratio** to [0, 2] before taking the mean, multiply by 100 and round to one decimal. This screen does not represent an uncapped physical capacity measurement.

<Callout type="warn" title="These are implementation equations, not validated process laws">The states are concentration surrogates, not ASM1 stoichiometric states or a reactor mass balance. MLD capacity is not reactor volume/HRT. The coefficients, weekly waveform and derived formulas have no supplied site calibration or independent validation; do not assign physical rate units/transferability without supporting evidence.</Callout>
